Design Notes

01 Surrogate keys

Generated at the dimension layer via dbt_utils.generate_surrogate_key(), matching the book's own end-to-end example. Facts join dimensions on sk_*, not natural keys like company_name — insulating joins from the exact identity-resolution fragility Section 3 uncovered.

02 Snapshot sources from staging, not a cleaned model

snap_crm_lead_lifecycle snapshots stg_crm_pipeline directly — lead_id is already a clean natural key, no dedup needed at that grain. dim_leads then joins the snapshot's output to int_crm_identity_resolved to attach company_name, keeping identity-resolution logic downstream of the snapshot as the book's pattern expects.

03 Mart and semantic layer, deliberately both

mart_growth_metrics_by_channel is the working, demoable v1. MetricFlow definitions sit alongside it as the governed next step — centralizing metric logic for every BI tool, once the hosted Semantic Layer (dbt Cloud) is in play rather than dbt-core alone.

04 plan_tier: noisy signal, resolved not discarded

Raw plan_tier/seats_used stay in staging. int_company_plan_tier_resolved mode-resolves a single company-level value with a confidence score, exposed on dim_companies — not trusted at the event grain, not thrown away either.
